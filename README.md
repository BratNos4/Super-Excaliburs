**Excalibur Mod**

**Excalibur Mod** adds two legendary swords to Minecraft: **Excalibur** and **Netherite Excalibur**.

* **Excalibur** is a powerful mid–late game weapon inspired by myth and crafted for players who want something truly special.
* For those who seek absolute endgame dominance, **Netherite Excalibur** takes the legend even further, combining the mythical blade with netherite power.

**Mod versions:**

* **Fabric:** 1.21.8 – 1.21.11
* **NeoForge:** 1.21.1 – 1.21.11
* **Forge:** 1.20.1
